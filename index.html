About the Poet

Amy Lowell — the voice behind the climb

Amy Lowell (1874 – 1925)

Amy Lowell was an American poet of the Imagist school, celebrated for her bold, vivid language and free-verse mastery. A leading figure in early 20th-century poetry, she championed precise imagery and emotional depth, often exploring nature, identity, and the human spirit.

Beyond her own work, Lowell was a tireless editor and critic who helped launch the careers of other modernist poets. Though her unconventional style drew criticism, she remained fiercely passionate — a trailblazer who reshaped American poetry.

Her poem "Climbing" exemplifies her signature style: rich metaphor, clear imagery, and a quiet yet powerful meditation on aspiration. Through the simple act of climbing a tree, she captures a universal truth about perseverance and the courage to reach higher.

"Poetry is the most concentrated form of verbal expression." — Amy Lowell

About the Poem

Understanding the journey upward

"Climbing" — A Deeper Look

"Climbing" is a beautifully crafted metaphorical poem that follows a speaker ascending an apple tree. On the surface, it tells the story of a child's determination to reach the top. But beneath that lies a profound allegory for the human journey toward our most cherished goals.


Each element of the poem carries symbolic weight:

🌳 Tree Life's challenges
🌿 Branches Steps of progress
🍃 Leaves Obstacles we push through
🏔️ Top Achievement & success
☀️ Sky Ultimate aspirations
🌍 Earth Where we started
✨ Core Message: The poem is a powerful anthem of perseverance — the refusal to give up, no matter how thick the leaves or how high the climb. It reminds us that every great achievement begins with a single step, and that the view from the top is always worth the struggle.

Section B: Multiple Choice

Click an option to see if it's correct!

a. What does the speaker view from the branch of the tree?

i. the sky ii. the town iii. the earth

✔ Correct Answer: ii. the town

b. What aids the speaker to go up and up?

i. the apple tree ii. the branches on the tree iii. a stair bought for her support

✔ Correct Answer: ii. the branches on the tree

c. What does the speaker compare the glow of a dome with?

i. a turret ii. a spire iii. sea foam

✔ Correct Answer: iii. sea foam

d. Which of the following statements is true?

i. The speaker used to climb onto the tree top. ii. The speaker is sure to get to the top now. iii. The leaves will prevent her from going higher.

✔ Correct Answer: ii. The speaker is sure to get to the top now.

e. Which statement carries the message of the poem?

i. We should not give up in the middle of any goal. ii. Be satisfied with what you have at the moment. iii. We cannot enter heaven without a staircase.

✔ Correct Answer: i. We should not give up in the middle of any goal.
